04 — Projects & Leadership

Things I've built & led

Featured Project

MyFinGrid

myfingrid.com

Production-grade personal finance platform built for India — full-stack, deployed live on Vercel. Spans 7 modules with a proprietary credit card engine, Gemini AI integration, Gmail bank-email parsing, and a pure TypeScript goals engine — all backed by 230 unit tests.

  • CredWise — Proprietary ENVS algorithm ranking 120+ Indian credit cards by net annual rupee value, with eligibility filtering, spend-weighted scoring, fee waiver achievability, and sign-up bonus amortization.
  • Goals Engine — SIP projection with monthly compounding, blended return calc, inverse SIP, and Gemini AI-backed recommendations. Covered by 159 unit tests.
  • Smart Import — 3 methods: CSV upload with column auto-mapping, Gmail OAuth scan parsing 15+ Indian bank email formats via Gemini AI, and SMS paste parsing.
  • Split & Share — Group expense splitting (equal, exact, %, shares) with settlement minimization, email invites via Resend, and rate limiting (15 invites/min).
  • Platform — 61 API routes · Row-Level Security (Supabase) · HSTS + CSP + Turnstile CAPTCHA · CI/CD auto-deploy on merge to main.
